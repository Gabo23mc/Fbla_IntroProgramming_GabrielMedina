Help:

To play the interactive games, follow these steps:

  • Choose a game from the menu.
  • Follow the on-screen instructions to navigate through the game.
  • Enjoy and have fun!

More tips for playing the games:

  • Turn on your sound, so you can hear music.
  • Wait some minutes, so you can enjoy some animations.
  • Try different games to see which one you like the most.

Tips for a good experience:

  • Use a modern and updated browser like Chrome, Firefox, or Edge for the best experience.
  • Close other heavy apps or browser tabs to improve performance and reduce lag.
  • Make sure your internet connection is stable.
